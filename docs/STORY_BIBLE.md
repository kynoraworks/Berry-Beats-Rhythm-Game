# Berry Beats: Story Bible and Build Prompt

Last updated 2026-11-20.

## Premise and tone

It is November in Berry Town. Berry, a quiet Blackberry in a purple hoodie, has barely left his room since the summer the trio split: he, Raspberry and Blueberry, best friends since childhood. Blueberry left for Fruit City and became one of its top dancers. Berry, asked to come along, said nothing, and has hidden ever since. When the Coach hands him a ticket to the Grand Dance at the end of the Line, Raspberry drags him onto the train. Stop by stop he builds a crew, and every fruit in Blueberry's crew says his dancing looks familiar. At the Grand Stage, a 5v5 crew battle ends in Berry vs Blueberry: two friends who learned the same moves, one who hid and one who showed off, and the first time Berry says what he never said.

**What split the trio.** In July, Blueberry was scouted for Fruit City and asked Berry to come. Berry froze. Raspberry, standing right there, froze too. Blueberry left, read the silence as a no, and grew arrogant. Berry hid. Raspberry has been angry at all three of them since.

**The Trio Turn.** As kids they invented a three-part move in Berry's garden: step-slide (Berry), spin (Raspberry), drop (Blueberry). Blueberry now does all three alone and built his name on it. That is why his crew keeps saying Berry's footwork looks familiar.

**Theme.** Berry hides, Blueberry shows off: two ways of being alone. Winning alone is the villain's move, so every win that matters is shared.

**Tone**

- Light, playful, a little cheeky, all ages, with one sincere core: a friendship that broke because nobody spoke.
- . Summer exists only as memory, like a faded photo of the trio and an overgrown garden
- Berry stays almost silent until stop 8, where his first full line lands. His biggest line is to Blueberry.

**World.** The Line is the train linking 12 stops. Fruit City is the last stop. The Grand Dance is its yearly contest, and entry needs a crew of five.

## Game systems

**Level loop (stops 2 to 11)**

- The train arrives at a station: one screen, themed to the fruit, with NPCs to talk to. The clue NPC must be talked to once before the exit opens.
- Walk outside to the district street: one wide screen, walk left and right, 3 or 4 clickable spots. The level's fruit waits at the end.
- Intro dialogue, then the battle, then win lines. A loss shows one retry line and restarts the battle.
- Back on the train. The route map shows the next stop.

Stop 1 is special: Berry's room (Coach tutorial, no battle), Raspberry's confrontation and battle, the ticket, then the station. Stop 12 is the 5v5 at the Grand Stage.

**Battle rules.** Keep the existing four-lane tug-of-war. You must win to progress. Win and lose never branch the plot: lose lines are retry banter only.

**Flair and abilities.** Hits fill a Flair meter (perfects fill more). At full, press Space to fire your fruit's ability for 8 beats, about 6.2 seconds at 77 BPM, then the meter resets. Berry has no ability: he anchors the crew. Every track is 77 BPM, so gimmicks are chart-based, never tempo-based.

**One mechanic per fruit.** The gimmick is what the fruit does to you in their level. If they join your crew, it flips into your ability.

| Stop | Fruit | Role | Gimmick against you | Ability for you |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Raspberry | Ally | Even chart, no gimmick (tutorial boss) | Steady: wider hit window |
| 2 | Banana | Local | Notes slide sideways into the next lane | none |
| 3 | Pear | Ally | Slow start, strong finish | Second Wind: double score in the back half of a round |
| 4 | Grape | Boss | Notes arrive in pairs | none |
| 5 | Dr. Lemon | Ally | Flawless streaks, then a meltdown scramble | Eureka: next 8 notes score triple, then a 2-beat scramble |
| 6 | Plum | Local | Long rests, patient patterns | none |
| 7 | Dragon Fruit | Boss | Flashes hide your arrows | none |
| 8 | Watermelon | Ally | Long hold notes | Rind: absorbs your next 3 misses |
| 9 | Coconut | Boss | Hard Shell: your Flair charges slower | none |
| 10 | Orange | Local | Rapid bursts, then rests | none |
| 11 | Pineapple | Boss | Misses cost double | none |
| 12 | Blueberry | Final | No gimmick, the hardest clean chart | none |

**Crew battle (stop 12).** Berry plus the four allies (Raspberry, Pear, Lemon, Watermelon) face Blueberry's crew: Grape, Coconut, Pineapple, Dragon Fruit, Blueberry. The player sets the order of the four allies. Berry always plays round 5 against Blueberry. The enemy lineup is visible in advance, so the order is a counter-pick.

- Five rounds on one shared tug-of-war bar. Rounds 1 to 4 are worth 1 point, round 5 is worth 2.
- Win with more than 3 of the 6 points. A 3-3 tie goes to whoever won round 5.
- Each fruit plays its own gimmick on its round. Allies can fire their ability once per round.
- A loss retries from the lineup screen.

## Arc: one line per stop

The 12 stops follow the Hero's Journey. The low point is stop 7, the center of the run. Allies and bosses alternate so neither bunches up.

| Stop | Station | Fruit | Journey stage | Arc line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Berry Town | Raspberry (ally) | Ordinary World, Call, Mentor | The Coach teaches the beat, Raspberry confronts Berry for hiding since summer, they battle, then the Coach hands over a ticket. Raspberry joins. |
| 2 | Peel Street | Banana (local) | Crossing the Threshold | First stop outside home. Berry barely speaks. A slapstick warm-up under a poster of Blueberry. |
| 3 | Graffiti Corner | Pear (ally) | Allies | Pear joins. A wall mural shows a step-slide that looks like Berry's. |
| 4 | Vine Crossing | Grape (boss) | Enemies | First of Blueberry's crew. Grape says the step is their captain's. |
| 5 | Citrus Labs | Dr. Lemon (ally) | Allies | Lemon joins, with footage of Blueberry's crew using Berry's footwork. |
| 6 | Plum Lantern Market | Plum (local) | Tests, false high | The crew's best night. Plum asks who is missing from Berry's dance. A scout in a dragon scarf takes notes. |
| 7 | The Club | Dragon Fruit (boss) | Ordeal, low point | Berry wins alone and the crew walks out because he never says what he feels. The crowd goes silent. |
| 8 | Rooftop | Watermelon (ally) | Reward | Berry speaks his first full line and admits what happened in July. The crew returns. Watermelon makes five. |
| 9 | The Tunnel | Coconut (boss) | The Road Back | Blueberry's crew blocks the line. Coconut says the captain never laughs anymore. |
| 10 | Orange Plaza | Orange (local) | Resurrection I | The Grand Dance opens. The crowd chants for Berry's face. He says not yet. |
| 11 | Pineapple Hall | Pineapple (boss) | Resurrection II | Pineapple reveals the captain keeps asking about a hoodie who never answered. |
| 12 | Grand Stage | Blueberry (final) | Return with the Elixir | 5v5. Berry vs Blueberry. Berry finally says yes, the hood comes down, the trio reunites. |

**Hint ladder.** Each stop moves the "your style is familiar" clue one step closer to the truth.

- Stops 2 and 3: a poster and a mural. Blueberry's step is everywhere, and it looks like Berry's.
- Stops 4 and 5: Grape and Lemon say it outright. It is the captain's step.
- Stop 7: Dragon Fruit says Berry wears it like an apology.
- Stop 9: Coconut says the captain stopped laughing.
- Stop 11: Pineapple says the captain asks about a hoodie who never answered.
- Stop 12: Berry answers.
