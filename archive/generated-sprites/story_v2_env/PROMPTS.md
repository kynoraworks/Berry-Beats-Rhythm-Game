# Story v2 environment art: prompts

Model `gpt_image_2_6`, quality high. Rooms, stills and props: 1k, 1024x640 (1.25 credits). Streets and the panorama: 2k, 2048x512 (2 credits).
Reference images are the existing art scaled up 4x with nearest neighbour (`station_berry_town`, `street_peel_street`, `room`, `stage_*`, the town's station and street).
Raw outputs are in `raw/<name>.png`; `tools/build_story_v2_env.py` turns them into the game files.

Shared phrasing (written out in full in every prompt): "Pixel art (interior) background for a 2D side-view game, in exactly the same chunky 16-bit pixel art style, pixel size and palette treatment as the reference images (flat colours, light dithering, dark outlines, no blur). Straight-on view of the back wall, no perspective, no people. Far left (3% to 14% of the width, floor up to half the height): [the door]. ... [floor] across the bottom 18%, clear for walking. [palette]. No text."
Below, each entry gives the refs and the part of the prompt that is specific to it.

## Cutscene stills
